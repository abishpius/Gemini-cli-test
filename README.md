# Gemini Village Simulator

![Gemini Village Simulator Gameplay](https://picsum.photos/seed/village/800/400)

A classic real-time strategy village-building simulator where you manage resources, construct buildings, and raise an army. Grow your village from a humble settlement with a single Town Hall to a bustling, fortified hub of military and economic power.

## ✨ Features

-   **Resource Management:** Balance the production and spending of five key resources: Wood, Food, Gold, Clay, and Iron.
-   **Building & Upgrading:** Construct and level-up a variety of buildings, each with unique benefits—from resource production and storage to unlocking and improving military units.
-   **Troop Training:** Raise an army of Swordsmen, Archers, and Villagers at the Barracks.
-   **Dynamic Queues:** Queue up multiple building constructions and batches of troops to streamline your village's growth.
-   **World Map & Combat:** Explore a persistent world map, scout nearby barbarian villages, and launch attacks to plunder their resources.
-   **Strategic Combat:** Troop stats, numbers, and a luck factor determine the outcome of battles. Higher-level Barracks train troops faster!
-   **Detailed Battle Reports:** Review the outcomes of your attacks with an inbox system that provides detailed reports on troop losses, resources looted, and more.
-   **Persistent Growth:** Your village continues to generate resources and progress through queues even when you're not actively playing (in-browser).

## 🚀 How to Play

1.  **Gather Resources:** Your initial buildings will provide a slow trickle of resources. Build a **Lumber Mill** and **Farm** to increase your Wood and Food production.
2.  **Expand Your Village:** Click on any building in the "My Village" panel to view its details, cost, and upgrade options.
3.  **Increase Storage:** Build a **Warehouse** to increase the maximum amount of each resource you can hold.
4.  **Raise an Army:** Construct a **Barracks**. Once built, you can train troops from the "Actions" panel. Upgrading the Barracks will speed up your training time.
5.  **Explore and Conquer:** Click the map icon (🗺️) to open the World Map. Find a red barbarian village, select it, and send your troops to attack and gather loot.
6.  **Check Your Mail:** After an attack, a battle report will be sent to your inbox (✉️). Check it to see how you fared!
7.  **Grow Your Power:** Continuously upgrade your buildings, expand your army, and become the most powerful village on the map!
